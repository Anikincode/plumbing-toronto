Toilet repair and installation in Toronto

Toilets are undeniably vital plumbing fixtures in any household. They are used multiple times daily, often without a second thought about their functionality. However, like any other appliance, toilets can deteriorate over time or suffer sudden damage, necessitating professional intervention for installation. In Toronto, residents seeking reliable toilet installation services can turn to our team at Plumbing Toronto for expert assistance. Whether you require a new toilet installation or are exploring other plumbing services, our dedicated team is ready to assist you.

Expert Toilet Installation Services

When you engage our professional plumbers at Plumbing Toronto for toilet installation, expect punctuality and comprehensive service from start to finish. Our licensed plumbers are trained to identify potential issues during installation, ensuring the safety and sanitation of your property. From unhooking the old toilet and removing it to installing the new unit and conducting necessary calibrations, we handle every aspect of the installation process with precision. Our goal is to leave you with a fully functional toilet and a clean workspace, minimizing disruptions to your daily routine.

Types of Toilets We Install

Contrary to popular belief, toilets come in various types, each offering distinct features and aesthetics. At Plumbing Toronto, our skilled plumbers are proficient in installing the following types of toilets:

  • Close-Coupled. The most common type, where the tank sits above and behind the bowl, offering easy access for maintenance.
  • Back-to-Wall. Featuring a concealed tank inside the wall or furniture, this type provides a modern, space-saving design.
  • Wall-Hung. With the tank concealed within the wall and the bowl suspended, this type offers a sleek, contemporary look and easy cleaning.

Flush Mechanisms

Choosing the right flush mechanism is crucial for efficient water usage and optimal performance. Our plumbers at Plumbing Toronto can advise you on various flush mechanisms available, including pressure-assisted, gravity-assisted, and dual flush options. By selecting the appropriate mechanism, you can conserve water without compromising on flushing power, resulting in cost savings and environmental benefits.

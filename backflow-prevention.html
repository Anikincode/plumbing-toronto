Backflow valve installation in Toronto

Backflow preventers are essential devices designed to safeguard your home's potable water supply from contamination. In the absence of a functional backflow valve, fluctuations in water pressure can lead to the backward flow of contaminated water through the pipes, carrying harmful substances with it. At Plumbing Toronto, your trusted local plumbing service, we specialize in all aspects of backflow preventer valve services, from installation and repair to testing and replacement.

Backflow Preventer Repairs

In many cases, various components of backflow preventers can be replaced, such as rubber gaskets, offering a cost-effective repair solution. However, if the backflow preventer valve is significantly aged or corroded, repairs may not be feasible. Our team at Plumbing Toronto will assess the condition of the system to determine if repairs are viable.

Backflow Prevention: common questions

What should I do if my backflow valve is leaking?

If you notice water leakage from a backflow valve, the first step is to shut off the water main to prevent further contamination and property damage. Then, promptly contact Plumbing Toronto to inspect and repair the valve.

Where are the typical backflow preventer locations?

Backflow preventers are commonly installed where municipal or well water enters the home. They are also frequently positioned at the intake point of irrigation systems.

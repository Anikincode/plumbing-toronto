Detecting and Repairing Slab Leaks

Slab leaks are among the trickiest plumbing problems to detect and fix. These leaks happen when pipes beneath your home's concrete slab or basement floor develop a leak.

Spotting a slab leak isn't always straightforward, but here are some signs to watch for:

  • The sound of running water when taps are off
  • Damp carpets, flooring, or concrete
  • Moist or moldy walls in your lower level
  • Musty odors in your basement
  • Surprisingly high water bills
  • Visible damage to your foundation

If you suspect a slab leak, it's crucial to act fast and seek professional water leak detection and repair services. Delaying repairs could lead to severe water damage and even structural issues.

A skilled plumber can locate the leak, create a minimal opening in your slab, complete repairs, and restore your slab to its original condition. Call Plumbing Toronto to have a suspected slab leak located and repaired.

Three common leak detection methods

  • Acoustic listening equipment. This gear amplifies the sound of water leaks in walls or under slabs, helping plumbers pinpoint the leak's location without unnecessary digging.
  • Video inspection equipment. Plumbers use tiny cameras to examine pipe interiors. By measuring how much cable they insert before finding the trouble spot, plumbers can diagnose pipeline issues and locate leaks accurately.
  • Soil probes. These devices help plumbers listen for pressurized water escaping into the soil around water mains or sewer lines, minimizing disruption to your yard and finding the leaky pipe.
